## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the project RERA registered?**
Yes. RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/2026/007841. Details can be verified on the Karnataka RERA portal.

**Q: What is the booking amount and is it refundable?**
₹2 Lakh, fully refundable if cancelled within 15 days of booking.

**Q: Can NRIs buy?**
Yes. NRIs can purchase with payments through NRE/NRO accounts. Power of Attorney execution is supported for registration.

**Q: Are the sizes negotiable / any offers?**
Launch offer (limited period): registration charges waived for the first 50 bookings in Tower A, and a ₹1.5 Lakh discount for spot booking on the day of site visit.

**Q: What are the maintenance charges?**
₹4.5 per sq ft per month on super built-up area, collected for 2 years in advance at possession.

**Q: Is Vastu compliance available?**
Yes, over 80% of units are east or north-east facing with Vastu-compliant entrances.

**Q: What about water supply?**
Cauvery connection applied for; borewells + treated water via in-house STP; tanker backup arrangement for the interim.

**Q: Site visit timings?**
Experience centre and model flat open all 7 days, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Free pick-up and drop within 15 km for scheduled visits.
